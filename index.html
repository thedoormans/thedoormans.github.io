Who We Are

The Doormans Limited is a Kenyan company dedicated to delivering quality products, reliable services, and sustainable growth opportunities. We believe in blending traditional business values with modern digital solutions.

Mission: To empower individuals, businesses, and communities with solutions that create real value, jobs, and digital inclusion.

Vision: To become a leading East African brand that integrates food, services, and digital ecosystems into everyday life.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Customer Success
  • Empowerment

Our Ecosystem: We collaborate with Ma Networld — a digital platform connecting people to earning opportunities, online services, and blockchain-powered growth. Together, we're building the future of community-driven business.
